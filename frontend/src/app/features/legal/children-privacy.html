Children's privacy notice

Many students who use the school's workspace are children. This notice explains how their personal data is handled and what parents and students can do about it. It should be read together with the Privacy policy.

Effective date
Privacy contact

1. The school is in charge

A school enrols its students and decides what information is kept about them. The school is the data fiduciary. The operator of the platform handles that information on the school's behalf and only as the school instructs.

2. Parents and consent

  • The school is responsible for telling parents what it collects and why, and for obtaining any consent the law requires before it collects a child's data.
  • Records of parental consent and of policy acceptance are planned and not yet in the app. Until they exist, the school should keep its own written record of consent.
  • Parents who sign in see only their own children's records. A parent account is linked to a student by the school.

3. Students' accounts

Student accounts are created by the school. The starting password set for a new account must be changed at the first sign-in. Schools should hand starting passwords over privately.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not track children's behaviour across websites or apps.
  • We do not show advertising to students or parents.
  • We do not build profiles of children for marketing, or for any purpose other than running the school's workspace.
  • We do not collect biometric data about children.

5. Sensitive identifiers

The platform does not store a Permanent Education Number (PEN) or an APAAR ID today. If a school enables them later, they will be treated as sensitive identifiers under the planned consent and access controls.

6. Who can see a child's record

  • A student sees only their own record.
  • A parent sees only the records of their own children, through their own sign-in.
  • Staff see only the students of their own branch, as their role allows.
  • Nobody outside the child's school can see the record.

7. Correction and erasure

Parents and students can ask the school to correct a record that is wrong, or to delete information the school no longer needs. The school decides what it must keep for its legal duties. To make a request, contact the school first. You can also write to the privacy contact above, and the request will be passed to the school. If it is not resolved, use the Grievances page.

8. Security

Children's records are protected by the same controls as all other data, described in the Privacy policy. Planned controls, such as the audit log and the data request tools, are listed on the Security page.

9. Changes

When this notice changes, the effective date above changes too.

Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data the Vidyasetu school management platform handles, why, who can see it and what you can do about it. It describes the platform as it is built today. Anything still planned is marked as planned.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 uses two terms here. The school is the data fiduciary: it enrols students and staff, decides why personal data is collected and how long it is kept. The operator is the data processor: it runs the platform and handles data for the school, on the school's instructions.

2. What data we handle

Depending on what the school enters, the platform handles:

  • Student records: name, date of birth, class and section, admission details, address and other enrolment information.
  • Guardian and parent details: name, relationship to the student, mobile number and email, and the parent's sign-in account.
  • Staff and account details: name, username, mobile number, email address, role and branch.
  • Attendance: daily attendance marks, leave and holiday records.
  • Fees: fee structures, invoices, payments recorded by staff, receipts and dues.
  • Notices: notices sent to a school, branch, class or section, and which users have read them.
  • Sign-in and security records: sign-in attempts, failed attempts and lockouts, kept to protect accounts.

We do not collect biometric data. The platform does not store a Permanent Education Number (PEN) or an APAAR ID today. Online payment is not yet available. When it is, the payment provider will handle card and bank details, and the platform will not store them.

3. Why we use it

  • To run the school's workspace: admissions, attendance, notices, fees and receipts.
  • To let staff, parents and students sign in and see only what they are allowed to see.
  • To keep accounts and the service secure.
  • To send messages to the people the school selects, only where the school has set up SMS or email.
  • To meet the record-keeping duties the school has under its own obligations.

We do not use personal data for advertising or marketing profiles, and we do not sell it.

4. Who can see it

Access depends on role, school and branch. The server checks each request against the signed-in user's role. The screens are not the only control.

  • A school owner can see all branches of their own school.
  • Branch staff (management, headmaster, teacher, accountant and clerk) see only their own branch, and only what their role allows.
  • A parent sees only their own children. A student sees only their own record.
  • One school never sees another school's data. One branch does not see another branch's data unless a school-wide role covers both.
  • Platform administrators run the service and can see data across schools in order to operate it. Their actions are not yet written to an audit log (planned).

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with advertisers. Optional services, such as SMS, email or file storage, are used only when they are set up with the provider's own credentials. Where they are not set up, nothing is sent to them. Otherwise, personal data is disclosed only where the law requires it.

7. How we protect it

These controls are in place today:

  • Passwords are stored as Argon2 hashes, never in readable form. A password must be at least 10 characters and must not be a commonly used password.
  • An account is locked for a set time after repeated failed sign-ins. The school sets the limits within fixed bounds.
  • Accounts created or reset by an administrator must change the password at first sign-in.
  • Sign-in and other sensitive requests are rate limited.
  • The access token is kept only in the browser's memory. The sign-in session uses an httpOnly, SameSite=Strict cookie that is rotated when it is used.
  • Responses carry security headers, including a content security policy that stops scripts from other sites from running.
  • Every request is limited to the signed-in user's school and branch on the server.

Some protections are planned and not yet in place. They are listed on the Security page.

8. Your rights

You can ask for:

  • access to the personal data held about you or your child;
  • correction of data that is wrong or out of date;
  • erasure of data the school no longer needs to keep;
  • a response to a complaint about how your data is handled.

The school decides why its records are kept, so send requests to the school first. You can also write to the privacy contact at the top of this page, and we will pass the request on to the school concerned. If a complaint is not resolved, use the Grievances page. Tracking requests inside the app is planned.

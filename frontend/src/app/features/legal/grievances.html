Grievances

Use this page to raise a complaint about how your personal data or your account is handled on the Vidyasetu platform. A complaint can be about a school's use of the platform, or about the operator's own handling of data.

1. Who to contact

Grievance officer
Email
Phone

2. Start with the school

The school decides how student and staff records are kept, so it is often the quickest place to start. The school's office or administration can correct a record, reset an account or explain a decision. If you prefer, or if the school does not resolve the matter, write to the grievance officer above.

3. What to include

  • Your name, and how you are connected to the student (parent, guardian or student).
  • The school and branch, and the username on your account if you have one.
  • What happened, and when. Describe what you expected instead.
  • Copies of any messages or screenshots that help, with anything that is not needed for the complaint removed.
  • What outcome you are asking for, for example a correction or deletion.

Do not send your password, one-time codes or copies of identity documents unless the officer asks for a specific document. We will never ask for your password.

4. What happens next

  1. We acknowledge your complaint.
  2. We look into it and may contact the school or ask you for more details.

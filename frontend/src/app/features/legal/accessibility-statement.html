{{ i18n.t('public.legal.accessibility.aim') }}

We build to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. The app is also meant to be understood on a small screen, in a browser zoomed to 200 per cent, and with reduced motion turned on.

{{ i18n.t('public.legal.accessibility.inPlace') }}

  • Every page has one main heading, and headings follow a logical order.
  • The page is divided into landmarks (header, navigation, main and footer), and each page has a "Skip to content" link.
  • Form fields have visible labels, and errors are shown next to the field they belong to.
  • Everything can be used with a keyboard, and a visible focus outline marks where you are.
  • Touch targets are at least 44 pixels on phones, tablets and touch screens.
  • Text colours are chosen for contrast with their background.
  • Motion is reduced when your device asks for it.
  • Layouts are designed for narrow screens first.
  • The interface is available in English and Marathi. The policy text itself is in English.

{{ i18n.t('public.legal.accessibility.limits') }}

We know of these gaps and are working on them:

  • The search box in the top bar is not available yet.
  • Some dense tables on wide desktop screens may need horizontal scrolling.
  • The app has not yet been tested with every combination of browser, operating system and screen reader.

{{ i18n.t('public.legal.accessibility.reporting') }}

If you cannot use part of the app, tell us. Describe the page, what you were trying to do, what device and browser you use, and what went wrong. Send it to the grievance officer on the Grievances page. You can also ask your school for help.

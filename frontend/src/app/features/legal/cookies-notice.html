Cookies and storage

This notice explains what the Vidyasetu web app stores in your browser and why. The app uses one cookie, for keeping you signed in. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Effective date

1. The sign-in cookie

When you sign in, the server sets a refresh cookie. It has these properties:

  • httpOnly: page scripts cannot read it.
  • SameSite=Strict: the browser sends it only for requests made from this site.
  • Lifetime: if you tick "Remember me", it is kept for a set number of days. If you do not, it is a session cookie and ends when you close the browser.
  • Rotation: each time it is used to renew your session, the server issues a new one and invalidates the old one.

Signing out removes the cookie and ends the session on the server. The cookie is needed to stay signed in, so if you block it you will have to sign in again each time you open the app.

2. Your access token

The short-lived access token that authorises each request is kept only in the memory of the open page. It is not written to local storage, session storage or a cookie. Reloading the page renews it from the sign-in cookie.

3. What we do not use

  • No analytics cookies or analytics scripts.
  • No advertising cookies, advertising identifiers or tracking pixels.
  • No third-party cookies.
  • No fonts, scripts or styles loaded from other websites. Fonts are part of the app.

4. Offline app shell

The app can be installed on your phone or computer. Its service worker stores the app's own files (the page, styles, scripts, icons and fonts) so that the app opens without a connection. The service worker does not store data from the school workspace, such as student, fee or attendance records. Those are always fetched from the server.

5. Controlling cookies and storage

You can delete cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. This signs you out. You can also sign out from the account menu in the app, which removes the sign-in cookie.

6. Changes

If the app starts using a new kind of cookie or storage, this notice will be updated and the effective date will change. We will not use cookies for advertising or tracking.
